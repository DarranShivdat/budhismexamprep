Key term strategy
For each of the 10 key term definitions, aim for 2–3 sentences structured as:

(1) Literal meaning — etymology or translation (e.g., "Anatman means 'no-self'…").
(2) Doctrinal role — what function does it play in Buddhist thought or practice?
(3) Textual/traditional anchor — connect it to a specific text, tradition, or figure from the course.

Terms most likely to appear: Anatman, Bodhisattva, Bodhicitta, Shunyata, Karma, Four Noble Truths, Upaya, Vajrayana, Vinaya, Arhat.
